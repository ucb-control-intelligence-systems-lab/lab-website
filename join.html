Thank you for your interest!

We receive a high volume of inquiries. To make sure we go through all of them, we have set up different contact paths for common requests and inquiries. Read below to see which one best fits you!

Please note that we cannot personally respond to all the inquiries that we receive. If you follow the paths below, rest assured that your inquiry has been received. We will be in touch if there is matching interest and availability!

Join us!

Graduate students

Apply directly to the Berkeley EECS Ph.D. program and mention Prof. Amo Alonso as a potential advisor in your application.

If applying in the fall of 2026, email Prof. Amo Alonso to discuss opportunities.

Undergrad students

If you are at UC Berkeley, share your transcript, CV, and projects that you are interested in! If we have a good fit, we will reach out to you.

Visitors
