The faces behind the papers

We are a team of researchers, students and academics committed to serving society through education and research in the EECS Department at UC Berkeley. We strive to cultivate a sense of belonging in a community centered around principled and controllable AI. In doing that, we support and actively celebrate the different perspectives that each of the members brings to the research process and the lab as a whole.

Director

Principal Investigator

Carmen Amo Alonso
Assistant Professor, Electrical Engineering & Computer Sciences, UC Berkeley

Carmen leads the Control and Intelligent Systems Lab as an Assistant Professor and the Lucy Hsu Faculty Fellow in EECS at UC Berkeley. Beyond her passion for academic research and teaching at a public university, Carmen has collaborated with teams at Google, Apple, and others. More recently, she engages in AI governance topics drawing on her engineering expertise. Committed to education for all, she travels to Latin America in the summers to teach underserved students.

Supporters

People and institutions that support our research

Academic Partners

We are lucky to have a wide range of collaborators across different academic institutions in the U.S., Europe and Asia, including partners in the UC system, Stanford, Princeton, MIT, Oxford, ETH Zurich, ELLIS Institute, Universidad Carlos III, TU Eindhoven, Osaka MU, and others.

Industry Relationships

Members of the lab have actively engaged with industry partners through internships and collaborations including the Modeling and Optimization Engineering Team at Tesla, the Machine Learning Research Team at Apple, and the Paradigms of Intelligence Team at Google, among others.

Funding Agencies

We are grateful to all of those who have partially funded our research! This includes UC Berkeley alumna Lucy Hsu and public and private entities, such as the Office of Naval Research, Schmidt Sciences, the ETH AI Center, Amazon AI4Science, among others.
